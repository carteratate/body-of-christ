# 5.2. Rights records for the Phase 5 sources

Item 5.2 in `P4-P5-republish-expand.md` (issue #195). Each planned work's public facts and
conclusion live in its row in `datapipeline/rights_inventory.json` (edition, translator, source URL,
status, renewal search, credit line, and `excerpt_basis` for an excerpt-only row). This file holds
the spec's other columns, which the inventory's closed field set has no place for: the site's
terms and the action taken, the size estimate, and the 5.2 status. It also keeps the running
storage total. Public facts only: the reasoning is in private memos (plan, Decision log "Rights
review").

Rows are researched in Phase 5's order: 5.3a, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6a, then 5.3b, 5.6b and 5.6c. A row stays a
draft (`checked_by` null) until Carter approves it.

**5.2 status**, from the row: *cleared* is a public-domain status (`pd-us-*`, `pd-dedicated`) or
`permission`, so the full reader; *excerpt-only* is `in-copyright` with an `excerpt_basis`;
*blocked* is `in-copyright` or `unknown` with no basis, which blocks its PR; *excluded* is a
work a decision keeps out, whose row stays for the record.

How the figures are made:
- **Text.** Characters of body text in each vendored or downloaded file, measured 16 Nov 2026
  (HTML and ThML without markup; ThML without `<note>`; Gutenberg without its header and licence;
  Internet Archive OCR text as is). A file shared by two rows is counted once. NPNF and OCR texts are
  taken at 85%, for the prolegomena, notes and page furniture each adapter strips. The 5.4 PDFs
  (Amoris Laetitia 357,506 characters, UDG 1996 80,035) were measured from vatican.va's files.
- **Passages.** Text divided by the live corpus's average passage length (16 Nov 2026): 1,200
  characters for Vatican documents (encyclicals 1,180), 2,300 for prose works (church-fathers 2,273,
  medieval 2,331), 400 for the Catena Aurea (one quotation per passage), and one passage per
  question for the Compendium of the Catechism (598).
- **Qdrant.** About 7.7 KB of disk per point (1,536 float32 values, 6,144 bytes; payload about 1.25
  KB, as 0.5 measured, 68 MB over 54,555 points; graph), plus 64 MB per collection for its
  write-ahead log and segments. A 2.2w publish builds a whole new collection beside the live one, so
  its peak is both collections. Live: one collection, `chunks`, 54,555 points, behind the alias
  `chunks_live` (read 16 Nov 2026).
- **Database.** 3.25 bytes per character of content (`chunks` 187 MB over 57.5 million characters
  after 4.0's rewrite). The transient staging copy of the collections a publish touches adds about
  as much again until it is dropped. Supabase disk was 36% of 8 GB on 30 Oct 2026; the database
  ends near 510 MB, far from 85%.
- **Sensitivity.** If new passages averaged the whole corpus's 1,055 characters instead, Phase 5
  would add about 104,000 passages and the last peak would be about 2.8 GB: still under the stop.
- **Carter to note (memory, not a stop).** The new collections keep vectors in memory (4.0 step 5).
  By 5.6c two collections hold about 0.68 GB of vectors each, more than the cluster's 1 GiB of RAM
  during a publish. Qdrant then reads vectors from disk, so search slows while the publish runs; it
  does not fail (4.0 step 5).
